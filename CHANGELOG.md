# Changelog

`.githooks/pre-push` refuses a tagged push unless the version in `pubspec.yaml`
matches the tag and has a heading here.

Written with [`.ai/prompts/release.md`](.ai/prompts/release.md) — user language,
not file names.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Browse 20 restaurants and 100 dishes, filter by category, cuisine, rating,
  delivery fee and distance.
- Add dishes to a basket, apply a promo code, and place an order.
- Watch the order move from confirmed to delivered, with a rider assigned when
  the kitchen hands it over.
- Sign in as a restaurant partner to work the incoming, in-kitchen and
  completed queues, and to edit the menu.
- Sign in as a delivery rider to go online, claim jobs, and update pickup and
  drop-off.
- Light, dark and system themes. Works offline against the seeded catalogue.
